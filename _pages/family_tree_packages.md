### Starter Family Tree

Not sure where to start? Perhaps the starter family tree package is for you.

This package will focus on two ancestors of your choice (usually grandparents),
and it will research their parents (your great-grandparents) and grandparents
(your great, great-grandparents). 

At the end, you will receive a family tree chart with the generations (your
grandparents, great-grandparents and, where possible, great,
great-grandparents).

**Price**: £350

### Traditional Family Tree

In this package, a surname of your choice will be researched back to the early
nineteenth century (around 1830 to 1840). 

To give you an idea, this usually includes four generations: a grandparent, a
great-grandparent, a great, great-grandparent and a great, great,
great-grandparent. Births, deaths, marriages will be included (i.e. year and
place), as well as any given occupations. Husbands/wives will also be included,
as well as siblings and children. This alone usually accumulates 30-odd
individuals, and we can discuss further options if you feel additional research
is needed.

At the end, you will receive a report with key information, transcripts of
documents/records and family tree charts. Official certificates will also be
included, with the price quoted including up to four purchases. If further are
required, I can either order them at an additional cost or provide the relevant
information for you to make the order in your own time.

**Price**: £800
