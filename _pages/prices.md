How much does it cost to hire a genealogist? 

The total quote will depend entirely on the size and complexity of your
project, however the prices below can be a useful guide.

- Ad hoc research is £50 per hour (chargeable time includes the research
  itself as well as writing reports, analysing material, and any time spent
  emailing, messaging and telephoning the client).

- Document transcription summary – starts at £50, though it depends on the
  length of the document. A quote will be sent to you via email and I will
  provide you with an estimate.
